Seven apps I build and use myself.

Two Mac apps, a database manager, two command-line tools, an email client and a strategy game. Most of them are open source, and most install with one line.

Install in one line

curl -fsSL https://sherin.dev/tagwell/install.sh | bash
  1. DownloadTagwell.zip
  2. ChecksumSHA-256 matches SHA256SUMS
  3. Installed/Applications/Tagwell.app

Installs Tagwell.app for macOS 15 or later. About Tagwell
