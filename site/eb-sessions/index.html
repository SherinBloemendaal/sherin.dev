EB Sessions: e-Boekhouden without the login friction.

A Chrome extension for the Dutch bookkeeping app e-Boekhouden.nl. Switch between administrations in one click, log in with 1Password, and get a login dialog instead of a lost page when a session expires.

What it does

Not released yet, and not on the Chrome Web Store. An unofficial tool, not made by or affiliated with e-Boekhouden.nl.

What it does

One click between administrations.

Every administration you logged in to stays one click away, and all e-Boekhouden tabs move over. No logging out and in.

1Password on the login page.

The login form moves out of e-Boekhouden's old frameset, so 1Password's inline menu works. Passwords never touch the extension.

Expired? Log in where you are.

A login dialog over the page instead of the login screen, and afterwards you are back where you were.

A calmer design, if you want it.

One switch turns on redesigned screens for logging in, importing bank lines, invoices, subscriptions and the archive.

It never books for you.

It only changes how pages look and work in your own browser. Every save and every submit is your own click.
