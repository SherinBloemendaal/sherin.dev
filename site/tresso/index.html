Tresso: do, hand over or know.

An email client built around one idea: every email is something to do, to hand over, or to know. Open source and self-hostable, for the web, the desktop and your phone.

The desktop app: macOS and Linux above, Windows below.

What it does

Do.

The email becomes a todo, linked to it, with a due date when there is one. Complete the todo and the email archives itself.

Hand over.

It goes to someone else with a note and a due date, and waits in Waiting on until they finish it.

Know.

It lands in the daily brief, cleared with one click on Noted.

One inbox for every account,

grouped into smart inboxes for todos, notifications, newsletters and invitations, plus your own.

Triage mode.

One email at a time, settled with D, H or K.

Everywhere.

Web, desktop and mobile, with notifications only for mail that needs you.
