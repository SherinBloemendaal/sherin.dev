WebRA: strategy in a browser tab.

A strategy game inspired by Command & Conquer: Red Alert and the open-source OpenRA engine, written in Rust and WebAssembly with WebGPU graphics. Nothing to install.

Play at webra.sherin.dev

What it does

Campaign.

The Allied and Soviet missions with their maps, rules and scripts.

Skirmish for 2 to 8 players,

free for all or in teams, against AI that plays OpenRA's own bots.

Online multiplayer.

Host a public or private game, with spectators, reconnecting and rematches.

Replays,

recorded for every game and kept in your account.

Map editor,

in the browser and on phones, and OpenRA maps can be imported.
